How the model decides

  1. Pump failure: the pump reports fault code 2. The countdown is exact physics: (shutdown − level) ÷ fill rate, with a p10–p90 band from the fitted level slope.
  2. Leak: CUSUM on pipeline pressure below its healthy value (disposal + 50 psi). Confirmed by pumped minus received flow.
  3. Worn pump: CUSUM on flow below rated, plus discharge pressure sag. A trend line predicts when the pump reaches 80% of rated flow.
  4. Cheapest plan: checks every mix of trucks × trips × wells to shut in × latest shut-in minute. The fleet is shared across a system's failures.
  5. Approve by: the optimizer is re-run at later start times; the deadline is the last one where the cheapest plan still works.
